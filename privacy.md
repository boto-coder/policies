# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 5 October 2026**

This policy describes what Scelestic.com does with information. It is a
general template. It does not create any obligation on Scelestic.com, and it
does not grant you any right to anything.

## The short version

Scelestic.com collects as little information as possible. The operator of this
site is a private individual, not a company. No warranty of any kind is given
about the accuracy of this document.

## Information this site may receive

A web server records technical data when you request a page. This data can
include your IP address, the page you asked for, the time of the request, and
the type of browser you used. This data exists only in ordinary server logs.

If you choose to send an email, the email you send and the address you send it
from are in the sender's mailbox.

If you run scripts, tools, or code from this site, that code runs on your own
computer. It does not report back to this site unless you configure it to.

## Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies. This site runs no analytics, no tracking beacons, and
no third-party advertising scripts.

If that changes, this page is the only place it is described. The operator is
not required to update this page.

## Third parties

Pages here may link to other websites. This site does not control those
websites and does not know what they collect. Read their own policies.

Some pages load fonts, images, or code from external services. Your browser
then contacts those services directly, and those services see your IP address.

## What is not done with information

Information is not sold. Information is not rented. Information is not shared
for advertising. The operator does not build profiles of visitors.

## Data retention

Server logs are deleted on a routine schedule, or when the machine is
reinstalled, whichever comes first. There is no guaranteed retention period and
no guaranteed deletion schedule.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws can give you rights over
personal data. This site does not know which laws apply to you, and it makes no
claim about which laws it complies with.

If you send a request about your data, the operator may respond, may not
respond, and may take any length of time to respond. There is no commitment to
a response time, and no commitment to remove anything.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. The operator does not knowingly
collect information from children. If information from a child is received, the
operator may keep it or delete it at their discretion.

## Security

No transmission method is guaranteed to be secure. The operator does not
guarantee the security of any information you send or any information stored on
this site.

## Cookies from third parties

Some content may include advertising or other content from third parties. The
third parties may use cookies to track you. The operator does not control these
cookies and is not responsible for them.

## Changes

This policy can change at any time. The date at the top shows the last change.
Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the new version. The
operator is not required to notify you of changes.

## Contact

There is no contact address published for this site, and none is required. This
document is published as general information only.
